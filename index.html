Inside the experiment

Built from a tetrahedral lattice. Distance and volume constraints keep the flesh elastic; the rind is a little firmer. Physics runs on the CPU at a fixed timestep using XPBD.

Native WebGPU and WGSL render the slice. Screen-space refraction, approximate thickness absorption, procedural membranes, and soft shadows create the jelly material. No downloaded models or images.

Readings come from the simulation, using a nominal mass of 420 g and 1 scene unit = 10 cm. This is an illustrative material experiment, not a calibrated physical measurement.

Drag empty space to orbit. Use the mouse wheel to zoom. Keyboard: N to nudge, R to reset, Space to pause.
